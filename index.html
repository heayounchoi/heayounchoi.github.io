Heayoun (Cindy) Choi

I am a Research Intern at the University of Toronto’s Medical Computer Vision and Robotics (MEDCVR) Lab, advised by Professor Lueder A. Kahrs. I received my M.S. in Computer Science & Engineering from Chung-Ang University, advised by Professor Eunwoo Kim at the AI and Robotics Lab.

My research focuses on generalization, autonomous adaptation, and physical-world reasoning for robots operating in changing and unfamiliar environments, with a long-term interest in autonomous exploration and sustainable habitats beyond Earth.
